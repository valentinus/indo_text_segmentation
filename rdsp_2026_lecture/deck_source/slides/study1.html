Part 2 · Research story

A text segmentation approach for automated annotation of online customer reviews

Hananto et al., 2022 — based on topic modeling

Challenges

Expert labelling is slow and expensive. Reviews are semantically noisy and often contain several topic-specific parts.

Research tasks

Build models and algorithms for multi-topic text classification, and test them for automatic annotation of online reviews.

0:20 — Introduce my own study. The core idea: if a review talks about three things, cut it into three pieces first, then label each piece. Human effort is limited to naming the topics.
