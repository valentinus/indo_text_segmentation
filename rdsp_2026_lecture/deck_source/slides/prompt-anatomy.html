A prompt is a codebook plus instructions

You are an annotator for a tourism research project.
The texts are YouTube comments on a travel video about Fushimi Inari Taisha (Kyoto), in English, Japanese or Indonesian.

Assign exactly ONE primary topic:
SCN = Scenery · CRW = Crowds · CUL = Culture · PRC = Practical · FDS = Food/shopping · VID = Video · OTH = Other
Give a secondary topic if clearly present, else "-".
Sentiment: POS / NEU / NEG. Sarcasm counts by its intended meaning.

Return a table: id | primary | secondary | sentiment | confidence | reason (max 12 words).
Do not skip, merge or invent comments.
