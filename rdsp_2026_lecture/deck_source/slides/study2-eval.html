0:30 — Highlight the human role. There are no gold-standard Indonesian summaries, so ROUGE or BERTScore could not be computed; human judgement is the gold standard for abstractive summaries anyway. Three experts independently ranked four versions of each of 30 summaries. Before trusting their rankings we measured their agreement — Kendall’s W = 0.547, the ranking counterpart of the κ you will compute in Activity 1. Result: summaries from transformer segments ranked best, statistically as good as gold sections and clearly better than the truncated whole article. Example (Belitung shipwreck): the whole-article summary missed key details; the segment-based summary named the 1998 discovery, the rope-bound Arab dhow and its Changsha ceramics. Bridge to Part 3: segmentation, summarization and evaluation are all things LLMs now attempt — but the evaluation still needed people.
