Agreement beyond chance: kappa

κ = (Pₒ − Pₑ) / (1 − Pₑ)

Pₒ observed agreement · Pₑ agreement expected by chance

Cohen’s κ: 2 annotators. Fleiss’ κ: 3 or more (your worksheet). Raw “% agree” looks better than it is: with 3 labels, random guessing already agrees often.
