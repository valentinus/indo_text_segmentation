What to take home

1

The pipeline is unchanged: collect, clean, label, model, interpret.

2

Labels are human decisions. A clear codebook matters more than the model.

3

LLMs handle short, multilingual, sarcastic text far better than keywords or LDA.

4

Always measure agreement with humans: κ is your quality certificate.
