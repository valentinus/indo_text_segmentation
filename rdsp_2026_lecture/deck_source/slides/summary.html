What to take home

1

NLP turns text into measurable data: collect, clean, label, model, interpret.

2

Generative AI changed the workflow: prompts replace much of the training.

3

Humans still define the labels. A clear codebook matters more than the model.

4

Always validate against humans: agreement (κ) is your quality certificate.

Challenge: pick any text you care about — reviews, posts, news — label 20 items yourself, run a codebook prompt, and report your human–AI κ.
