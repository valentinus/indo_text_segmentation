Limits

LLM annotation needs a human in the loop

RiskWhat it looks likeCheck
Hallucinationquotes or counts that are not in the datarow count = input; quotes must exist
Inconsistencylabels flip between runs or modelsrun twice; send flips to a human
Biascultural or language-specific misreadingssample per language; native-speaker review
Leakage of trust“the AI said so” replaces validationκ against a human-labelled sample
Privacy & termsusernames or personal data in promptsanonymize; follow platform terms and ethics review

Rule of thumb: humans define the labels and audit a sample; the AI does the volume.

1:19 — Same principle as the 2022 “bronze standard”: automated labels are useful if their agreement with humans is measured and reported. In a thesis, report κ between LLM and human annotators, the prompt, the model version and the date.
