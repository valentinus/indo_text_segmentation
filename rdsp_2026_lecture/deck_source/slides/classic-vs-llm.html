1:11 — These numbers come from run_experiments.py. Be honest about the seed-word result: I wrote the keyword lists after reading the comments, so 0.91 is optimistic; on new data it would drop. Even so it fails on other languages and on sarcasm (comment 13 was labelled VID because of the word “video”). LDA needs long documents; on 60 short, multilingual comments its topics are noise — “banget”, “pagi”, “ramai” are Indonesian words mixed into English topics. Note: agreement with an LLM is not accuracy; the LLM labels are themselves a hypothesis to check against humans.
