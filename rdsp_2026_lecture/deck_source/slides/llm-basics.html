Part 3 · Generative AI

What is a large language model?

1

Pretraining

Learn to predict the next word from trillions of words of text. Grammar, facts and style emerge from this one task.

2

Instruction tuning

Further training on examples and human feedback teaches the model to follow instructions and answer helpfully.

3

Prompting

You describe the task in plain language. Zero-shot: no examples. Few-shot: a handful of examples in the prompt.

An LLM generates the most plausible continuation — not a verified fact. That is both its power and its main risk.

0:31 — Keep it conceptual. Pretraining gives general language ability; instruction tuning makes it usable; prompting replaces task-specific training for many problems. Stress the last line: plausible is not the same as correct, which is why we will validate LLM output against humans in Activity 2.
