Segment first, then summarize

Hananto, Santoso & Nurcahyawati · Transformer-based text segmentation for Indonesian · ICACS 2025

Data · 60,798 Indonesian Wikipedia articles; section headings give the gold boundaries

Benchmark · 10 methods: classical, topic-model and transformer. Best: Congen-IndoBERT, Pk 0.30 (TextTiling 0.47)

Downstream + people · segments go to an abstractive summarizer (BRIO); humans judge the summaries

0:28 — My most recent paper, and the link between the two halves of this lecture. Long documents exceed the input limit of summarization models, so we cut them into coherent segments first and summarize each segment. Walk the figure: preprocessing, ten segmentation methods (from TextTiling to transformer embeddings), evaluation with Pk and WindowDiff against Wikipedia section boundaries, then the downstream task. Note the box at the bottom of the figure: user evaluation. Transformer embeddings won; the language-specific Congen-IndoBERT matched the multilingual Qwen3 model at one fifth of the runtime (about 1 hour vs 5).
