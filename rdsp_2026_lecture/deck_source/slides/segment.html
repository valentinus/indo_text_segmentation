TopicDiff-LDA: cut where the topic changes

Slide a boundary

Move a candidate boundary b sentence by sentence through the review.

Compare topics

Infer LDA topic distributions left and right of b; measure their difference.

Cut and label

Split where the difference peaks; give each segment its top topic.

0:21 — Most segmentation methods give unlabelled segments or are costly. TopicDiff-LDA uses the topic model it already has, so segments come with labels for free. Keep the maths light.
