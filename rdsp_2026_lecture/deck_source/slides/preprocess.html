Preprocessing and features on one comment

“The rows of gates feel like walking through a tunnel of fire. Absolutely breathtaking!!”

StepResult
Normalizethe rows of gates feel like walking through a tunnel of fire absolutely breathtaking
Tokenize[the, rows, of, gates, feel, like, walking, …]
Stopwords out[rows, gates, feel, walking, tunnel, fire, absolutely, breathtaking]
Lemmatize[row, gate, feel, walk, tunnel, fire, absolutely, breathtaking]
Bag of words{gate: 1, tunnel: 1, fire: 1, breathtaking: 1, …} — order is lost
TF-IDF“tunnel”, “fire” score high (rare); “gate” lower (in many comments)

Note: “fire” is a metaphor here. Bag-of-words cannot see that; context-aware models can.
