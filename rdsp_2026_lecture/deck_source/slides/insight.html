From labels to decisions

Data → information → insight for the tourism office

Data

60 labelled comments, 7 topics × 3 sentiments

Information

Crowds: 9 of 10 negative. Scenery: 10 of 11 positive. Tips repeat “go early”, “go higher”.

Insight

The experience people love exists — at off-peak times and on the upper trail. The problem is concentration, not the place.

1 · Spread visitors

promote early-morning visits and the trail beyond Yotsutsuji

2 · Etiquette in 3+ languages

culture and behaviour complaints overlap (photos, eating while walking)

3 · Keep listening

re-run the pipeline monthly on real comments; track the crowd share
