Annotation is the bottleneck

Manual annotation

  • experts read and label every text
  • slow and expensive
  • unreliable when a text has several topics

Automatic classification

  • a model trained on labelled data
  • fast, cheap per text
  • low precision on multi-topic texts — semantic noise

Both routes start with the same question: who decides the labels, and how do we know they are right?

0:13 — This is the motivation of my 2022 study. A classifier is only as good as its training labels, and human labels are costly and noisy, especially for reviews that mix several topics.
