Opening question

One travel video, 800 comments. What are visitors telling Kyoto?

Positive?

“Went at 6am and had the whole mountain almost to myself.”

Negative?

“Great, another ‘hidden gem’ video that will bring 10,000 more people tomorrow 🙄”

Topic?

外国人観光客が多すぎて、地元の参拝客がゆっくりお参りできないのが残念。

“Too many foreign tourists — a shame local worshippers can’t pray in peace.”

Reading 800 comments at 5 seconds each takes over an hour — and two readers will not even agree on what they mean.

0:01 — Read the three comments aloud. Ask: which is positive? Is the second one positive because it starts with “Great”? What is the third one about — crowds, culture, or both? Point: understanding text at scale needs NLP, and even humans disagree. That disagreement is the thread of today’s lecture.
