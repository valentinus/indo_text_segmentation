Opening question

People write millions of reviews, posts and comments every day. Who reads them?

Positive or negative?

“Battery lasts two days, but the camera is a real disappointment.”

What is meant?

“Great, another update that breaks everything 🙄”

What is it about?

外国人観光客が多すぎて、地元の参拝客がゆっくりお参りできないのが残念。

“Too many foreign tourists — a shame local worshippers can’t pray in peace.”

Understanding text at scale needs machines — and even two humans do not always agree on what a sentence means.

0:01 — Read the three texts aloud. The first is mixed: positive and negative at once. The second starts with “Great” but is sarcastic. The third is in Japanese and touches two topics, crowds and religion. These are the everyday problems of NLP: sentiment, sarcasm, topic, language. And humans disagree about them too — that disagreement is the thread of today’s lecture.
