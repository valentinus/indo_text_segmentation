Topic modeling: letting the data propose the labels

LDA (Latent Dirichlet Allocation) is a generative probabilistic model:

  • a document is a mixture of topics
  • a topic is a distribution over words
  • each word is drawn from one topic

The number of topics is chosen by experiment, e.g. with coherence scores. Humans then name each topic.
