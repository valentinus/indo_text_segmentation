One text, one label — or three?

Watu Dodol, Banyuwangi (East Java): a 14-sentence tourism description

Baseline LDA

Beach (0.32)

The whole text gets one label; natural place (0.25), things to buy (0.15) and culture (0.13) are lost.

TopicDiff-LDA

Natural place (0.54) — location, distance, panoramic ocean, view of the Bali strait

Things to buy (0.34) — culinary activities, shell souvenirs, the Gandrung statue

Historical sites (0.33) — legend of the rock, Japanese occupation in WWII

0:24 — Same text, two methods. The baseline forces one label; the segmented version recovers three meaningful parts. Keep this example in mind: many YouTube comments are also multi-topic, just shorter.
