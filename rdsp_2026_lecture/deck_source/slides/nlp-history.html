Part 1 · Foundations

Seventy years of teaching machines language

1950s–80s

Rules

hand-written grammars and dictionaries; ELIZA (1966)

1990s–2000s

Statistics

n-grams, Naive Bayes, SVM; topic models such as LDA (2003)

2013

Embeddings

Word2Vec, GloVe: words become vectors that carry meaning

2017–2020

Transformers

attention; BERT, GPT, T5; pretrain once, fine-tune per task

2022–

Generative AI

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini: one model, many tasks, plain-language prompts

Each era moved more of the work from people writing rules to models learning from data. Today’s lecture spans the last three.
