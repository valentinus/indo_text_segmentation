Part 3 · Generative AI

One model, many NLP tasks

Classification

sentiment, topic, intent, spam

Information extraction

names, places, dates, prices, product features

Summarization

long reports, news, meeting notes, reviews

Translation

including low-resource and mixed-language text

Question answering

over your own documents (retrieval-augmented generation)

Generation & rewriting

drafting, paraphrasing, simplifying, synthetic data

Before 2022, each task usually needed its own labelled dataset and its own model. Now one general model can attempt all of them — but someone still has to check the results.
